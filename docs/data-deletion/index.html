67 Seconds for Android

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 1, 2026

67 Seconds does not provide user accounts and does not store gameplay progress on DyxotaGame servers.

Delete local gameplay data

Your best survival time, completed-round count, language, audio, vibration, and advertising privacy settings are stored locally on your Android device. DyxotaGame cannot remotely access or delete this data. You can delete it immediately by either:

  • opening Android Settings → Apps → 67 Seconds → Storage → Clear storage; or
  • uninstalling 67 Seconds from your device.

Request deletion from DyxotaGame

  1. Email dyxotapls@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject 67 Seconds Data Deletion Request.
  3. State whether your request concerns support correspondence, advertising data, or both.
  4. Include only the information reasonably needed to locate relevant records. Do not send passwords, payment details, or government identifiers.

We may reply to verify that the request came from the email address associated with the relevant correspondence. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days unless applicable law permits or requires more time.

Data that can be deleted

  • Support correspondence: messages and attachments you sent to DyxotaGame that remain under our control.
  • Advertising-related requests: we will assist with a request involving Unity LevelPlay and the Game’s configured mediated networks, Unity Ads and Yandex Ads. We may direct you to the relevant provider because DyxotaGame does not control information retained in a provider’s systems.

Data that may be retained

After a verified deletion request is completed, residual copies of support correspondence may remain in restricted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten. We may retain limited records longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Advertising information controlled by Unity, Yandex, or another applicable advertising partner is subject to that provider’s retention practices and legal obligations. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the Yandex Ads Privacy Policy for provider-specific information and privacy request options.
